---
name: clarify-ambiguous
title: Ambiguous or underspecified request
triggers: it, that thing, make it better, improve, do the usual, something like, you know what I mean, fix it up
examples:
  - "make this better"
  - "can you do the thing we talked about"
  - "improve the file"
applies_when: the request could mean several different tasks and the difference matters
---
**Done looks like:** one precise question that resolves the ambiguity — or a stated assumption plus a quick first version the user can correct.
**Approach:** check context first (open checklist, recent files, memory_search for earlier preferences); if one reading is far more likely, proceed on it and say so; otherwise ask one question with 2–3 concrete options.
**Tools:** memory_search; list_dir / read_file to see what "it" probably is.
**Pitfalls:** long clarification lists; silently picking an interpretation on a costly or irreversible task; asking what the context already answers.
**Verify:** the assumption is written in your reply.
**Stop / ask:** always, when the readings lead to different deliverables and the work would be long.
