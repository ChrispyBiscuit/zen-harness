---
name: infeasible-request
title: Request that cannot be fully satisfied as asked
triggers: cannot, not possible, blocked, requires login, private data, no access, paywall, doesn't exist publicly
examples:
  - "get me everyone's private phone numbers from a site"
  - "find the internal document of a company"
  - "download a paid report for free"
applies_when: what is asked for is not publicly available, needs access you do not have, or is not allowed
---
**Done looks like:** a clear statement of what is and is not possible, the closest legitimate substitute delivered or offered, and a question about how to proceed.
**Approach:** confirm the obstacle quickly (one search / one attempt, not ten); identify what *is* available (public summaries, official channels, anonymised sources, the user's own accounts); do the feasible part; explain the rest in two sentences.
**Tools:** whatever the feasible part needs; nothing to work around access controls.
**Pitfalls:** repeating the same blocked attempt with new wording; presenting a substitute as the real thing; giving up without offering the substitute.
**Verify:** the user can tell exactly which items are the substitute.
**Stop / ask:** as soon as the obstacle is confirmed — before spending more steps.
